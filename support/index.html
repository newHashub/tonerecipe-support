界面语言

ToneRecipe 支持简体中文和英文，界面跟随 iOS 中的 App 语言设置。文字参数和截图识别均支持中英文参数名称。App 语言与购买所用的 App Store 地区是独立设置。

无法选择照片或截图

  1. 在系统照片选择器中重新选择要导入的照片或截图。
  2. 若 App 提示 Live Photo 读取权限不足，请打开“我的 → 照片访问权限”，在系统设置中允许访问该照片。
  3. 返回 ToneRecipe 后重新导入。

ToneRecipe 使用 Apple 系统照片选择器，不会浏览或上传你的完整相册。

参数识别不完整

  • 裁剪到参数区域，避免文字过小、模糊或强反光。
  • 尽量使用高对比度、每行一个“参数名 + 数值”的截图。
  • 应用前检查低置信度、范围截断和未识别内容。
  • 在编辑器中修正近似或不适合当前照片的值。

Live Photo 没有播放或导出

请确认导入的是系统相册中的原始 Live Photo，并允许 ToneRecipe 读取照片资源。点击编辑器上方的 Live 标识可播放一次。导出时保持 App 在前台，等待动态资源写入完成。

使用 App 内相机拍摄时，麦克风权限仅用于保留 Live Photo 的现场声音；拒绝后仍可拍摄普通照片。

预览或导出失败

检查设备剩余空间和相册写入权限，等待预览完成后再重试。取消或失败的导出不会消耗免费次数。删除 ToneRecipe 中的最近记录不会删除相册中的已导出副本。

免费额度或 Pro 状态异常

免费版每个日历月包含 5 次成功导出；参数识别、参考图匹配、编辑、预设和最近记录不限制次数。

在 ToneRecipe Pro 页面选择“恢复购买”，并确认设备联网且登录了购买时使用的 Apple 账户。永久版不会自动续费。

删除本地数据

在“最近”中删除记录，在预设详情中删除自定义预设，在系统“照片”中单独删除已导出的照片。卸载 App 会移除 App 容器中的其余数据，但 iOS 可能保留钥匙串中的免费导出计数。

Help & Support

ToneRecipe supports English and Simplified Chinese. The interface follows your iOS app language setting. Text recipes and screenshot recognition support parameter names in both languages. Your App Store purchase region does not determine the interface language.

简体中文帮助 · Privacy and photos containing faces

Importing a photo or screenshot

Choose the image again in the system photo picker. If Live Photo resources cannot be read, open Profile → Photo access and allow access to that photo in Settings, then import it again. ToneRecipe processes content you choose; it does not upload your library. Downloading an original from iCloud Photos may require a connection.

Incomplete recognition

Use a clear, high-contrast screenshot cropped to the settings, ideally with one parameter per line. English and Chinese parameter names are supported. Use integer slider values, such as Exposure +10 and Contrast -15. Decimal, EV, Kelvin, fractional and scientific notation are not automatically converted. Global Hue is not Tint; channel-specific HSL Hue must identify a color channel. Review unsupported lines, low-confidence values and range limits before applying.

Reference-photo analysis estimates a starting recipe; it does not recover the original editing settings or guarantee an identical result. ToneRecipe uses its own rendering scale, so identical numbers need not match Apple Photos or another editor.

Live Photo playback or export

Import the original Live Photo. Tap LIVE in the editor to play one cycle. Keep the app in the foreground until export finishes. Camera microphone access is used only to preserve sound in captured Live Photos; without it you can take still photos. HDR preservation is not currently supported.

Preview or export failures

Check available storage and permission to add photos, wait for the preview, then retry. Canceled and failed exports do not use your free allowance. Deleting a recent project in ToneRecipe does not delete exported copies in Photos.

Free exports and Pro Lifetime

The free version includes five successful image exports per calendar month. Recognition, Photo Match, editing, presets, and recent projects remain available without usage limits. Pro Lifetime removes the export limit through a one-time purchase with no automatic renewal.

To restore, open ToneRecipe Pro → Restore Purchases while online using the Apple Account that made the purchase. This explicitly synchronizes with Apple and may request account verification. Apple determines the displayed purchase currency and final price.

Deleting local content

Delete projects in Recents, custom presets in Preset Details, and exported images separately in Photos. Uninstalling removes the app container, but iOS may retain the Keychain free-export counter. iCloud Photos and device backups follow your Apple settings.
